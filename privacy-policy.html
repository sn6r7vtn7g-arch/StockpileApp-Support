StockpileApp Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026

This page is provided in English first for App Review and international users. The Chinese version is included below.

Introduction

Welcome to StockpileApp. We understand the importance of personal information security. This policy explains how the app collects, uses, stores, and protects your information.

Information Collection and Use

1. Information You Provide

  • Baby profile information: baby name, birth date, gender, weight, and related profile details, used only for in-app records and recommendations
  • Inventory information: item name, category, stock quantity, model, price, and related inventory records, used only for local inventory management
  • Family information: family name, members, invitation codes, and sharing status, used only for family sharing features inside the app

2. iCloud Data Sync

  • When iCloud is enabled, data syncs between devices through Apple CloudKit
  • Synced data is stored in your iCloud account or the accepted CloudKit shared database; the developer cannot access your private iCloud data
  • Data transfer and storage are protected by Apple's iCloud and CloudKit security mechanisms

3. Local Storage

  • When iCloud is not enabled, app data is stored only on the device

4. Device Permissions

  • Camera: used only for taking baby profile photos and milestone photos
  • Photo Library: used only for selecting baby profile photos, milestone photos, and videos
  • Notifications: used for local inventory, vaccine, weight, daily care, and restock reminders; iCloud sync may use Apple CloudKit system background notifications required by Apple's framework

5. E-commerce Link Import

  • E-commerce link import is used only to help identify item names, source platforms, and possible quantity information
  • Import requests are limited to allowed e-commerce domains, use an ephemeral network session without cookies, and limit response size
  • Please do not paste passwords, payment details, ID numbers, or other sensitive information into import text

6. Unused System Capabilities

  • The app does not use HealthKit or CareKit APIs
  • The app does not use NFC and does not require external hardware pairing
  • The app does not operate a custom APNs server and does not store device tokens; user reminders are scheduled locally on the device

Data Security

Data storage and synchronization rely on Apple's local storage, iCloud, and CloudKit security mechanisms. We do not collect analytics data or crash logs, and we do not share your data with third parties.

Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, or social media SDKs.

Data Deletion

You can delete individual data records inside the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes local app data from the device.

Children's Privacy

Baby and child-related data should be entered and managed by parents or legal guardians. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 14.

Contact Us

For questions, contact: Stockpilebaby@outlook.com

宝宝囤（StockpileApp）隐私政策

最后更新：2026年6月2日

引言

欢迎使用宝宝囤。我们深知个人信息安全的重要性，本政策说明我们如何收集、使用和保护您的信息。

信息收集与使用

1. 用户主动提供的信息

  • 宝宝档案信息：宝宝姓名、出生日期、性别、体重等，仅用于 App 内记录和推荐
  • 物品信息：物品名称、分类、库存数量、型号、价格和相关库存记录，仅用于本地库存管理
  • 家庭信息：家庭名称、成员信息、邀请码和共享状态，仅用于 App 内家庭共享功能

2. iCloud 数据同步

  • 启用 iCloud 后，数据通过 Apple CloudKit 在多设备间同步
  • 同步数据存储在您的 iCloud 账户或已接受的 CloudKit 共享数据库中，开发者无法访问您的私人 iCloud 数据
  • 数据传输和存储受 Apple iCloud 与 CloudKit 安全机制保护

3. 本地存储

  • 未启用 iCloud 时，App 数据仅存储在设备本地

4. 设备权限

  • 相机：仅用于拍摄宝宝头像和成长里程碑照片
  • 相册：仅用于选择宝宝头像、成长照片和视频
  • 通知：用于本地库存、疫苗、称重和日常提醒；iCloud 同步会使用 Apple CloudKit 所需的系统后台通知

5. 电商链接导入

  • 电商链接导入仅用于帮助识别物品名称、平台来源和可能的数量信息
  • 导入请求限制在允许的电商域名，使用不保存 Cookie 的临时网络会话，并限制响应大小
  • 请不要在导入文本中粘贴密码、付款信息、身份证号或其他敏感内容

6. 未使用的系统能力

  • 本 App 不使用 HealthKit 或 CareKit API
  • 本 App 不使用 NFC，也不需要外部硬件配对
  • 本 App 不使用自建 APNs 服务端，不保存 device token；用户提醒均由设备本地调度

数据安全

数据保存和同步依赖 Apple 提供的本地存储、iCloud 和 CloudKit 安全机制。我们不收集分析数据或崩溃日志，不分享数据给任何第三方。

第三方服务

本 App 不集成任何第三方分析、广告或社交媒体 SDK。

数据删除

您可在 App 内随时删除单个数据；卸载 App 即可清除设备上的本地 App 数据。

儿童隐私

儿童相关数据应由父母或法定监护人输入和管理。我们不有意直接收集 14 岁以下儿童的个人信息。

联系我们

如有问题请联系：Stockpilebaby@outlook.com
